## How I work

Every project here follows the same loop, the way an analyst works with a team:

1. **Business question first**: who is asking, and what decision changes?
2. **Clean and validate**: document every data issue and how I handled it.
3. **Reconcile**: key numbers are checked across SQL, Excel and Power BI before I trust them.
4. **Recommend**: findings end in prioritised actions, plus a **Limitations** section on what the data cannot prove.

## Featured projects

### [SaaS Revenue, Churn & Customer Risk Analysis](https://github.com/Muhammad-Amjad-DA/saas-revenue-churn-analysis)
`MySQL` `Excel` `Power BI` `DAX`

600 customers, 48 months of revenue. Where is churn concentrated, what does it cost, and which active customers should Customer Success call first?

| Finding | Result |
|---|---|
| Historical churn | **52.17%** (313 of 600) |
| Starter vs Enterprise churn | **70.51%** vs **22.00%** |
| Monthly vs annual billing churn | **60.51%** vs **40.32%** |
| Active high-risk accounts flagged | **31** |

Includes documented KPI definitions, a data dictionary, SQL / Excel / Power BI reconciliation, and honest limitations. *(Synthetic dataset, used to demonstrate workflow.)*

### [Retail Sales SQL Analysis](https://github.com/Muhammad-Amjad-DA/SQL-Data-Analysis)
`PostgreSQL` `CTEs` `Window functions`

A 4-table relational schema and 9 business questions answered with joins, CTEs, `RANK`, `LAG` and running totals. Includes a recency-frequency-spend segmentation case study: **~40% of customers drove ~74% of revenue**, and a recommendation on where to spend a limited retention budget.
