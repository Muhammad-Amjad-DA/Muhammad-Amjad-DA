## How I work

1. **Business question first**: who is asking, and what decision changes?
2. **Clean and log**: every data issue, how many rows, and what I decided.
3. **Reconcile**: headline numbers pass SQL tie-out tests before I quote them.
4. **Recommend, with limits**: findings end in actions, impact scenarios with stated assumptions, and a list of what the data cannot prove.
5. **AI, documented**: each project has an AI log of what the assistant did, what it got wrong, and what I decided.
